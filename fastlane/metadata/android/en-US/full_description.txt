Flint is a fast, modern video player for Android, powered by the libVLC engine and wrapped in a clean Jetpack Compose and Material 3 interface. It keeps VLC-grade playback and drops the clutter: gesture-first controls, a quick local library, no ads, no tracking and no accounts.

PLAYBACK
• Plays almost any container and codec libVLC can open (MP4, MKV, AVI, WebM, TS and more)
• Hardware decoding first, with an automatic software retry from the same spot if a decoder fails
• Decoder modes: Auto, Software only or Hardware+
• Playback speed, aspect ratio (fit, stretch, crop) and orientation lock
• Queue with shuffle and repeat, resume where you left off
• Picture-in-picture and background playback with a media notification
• A-B repeat, pinch to zoom up to 5x, clean video screenshots

SUBTITLES AND AUDIO
• Embedded and external subtitles, auto-converted to SRT (WebVTT, ASS/SSA, TTML, SAMI, MicroDVD and more)
• Subtitle size, color and outline, shadow or box style
• Multiple audio tracks, audio delay and subtitle delay
• 10-band equalizer with presets, pre-amp and volume boost up to 200%

GESTURES
• Swipe to seek, brightness on the left, volume on the right
• Double-tap zones to skip or play and pause
• Screen lock against accidental touches
• Sleep timer with a gentle fade-out

LIBRARY
• Folder browsing and an all-videos view, with thumbnails cached off the main thread
• Search, sorting, playlists and history
• Choose which folders are scanned
• Rename, share and delete from the context menu

NETWORK
• Save SMB, FTP, SFTP and NFS servers and play straight from your NAS, PC or router
• Finds SMB, NFS, FTP and UPnP / DLNA servers on your Wi-Fi
• Open HTTP(S), HLS, DASH, RTSP, RTMP, MMS, UDP and RTP streams
• Open M3U, PLS and XSPF playlists from the web
• Casting to TVs and speakers found on your network
• Saved logins stay on your phone

LOOKS
• Material 3 with dynamic color from your wallpaper, or any custom color
• Dark, light and pure black themes
• Material or Glass interface style
• Classic or Glass player controls, 4 seekbar styles and 4 slider styles
